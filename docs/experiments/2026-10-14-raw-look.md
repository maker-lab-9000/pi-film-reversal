# A look trained on the DNG, against today's look

Date: 2026-10-14. Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-raw-look-design.md`
(phases 1 and 2). Plan for phase 1: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-14-raw-developer.md`.

**Result: no gain, not pursued.** A look trained on developed DNGs, like for like
with the deployed look, came out visually the same as today's. The owner judged
the gallery: "visually I see no gain"; the cost (developing every photograph on the
Pi, probably several seconds each) is not worth it. The deployed look stays
(`imx477-2026-09-v4-dark`, tile grain) and the Pi no longer saves DNGs
(`--no-dng`, from 2026-10-14). The developer (`pifilm/raw.py`) stays in the code,
so DNGs already taken can still be developed.

## Phase 1: developing the DNG

`pifilm/raw.py` reads Picamera2's DNG (12-bit packed Bayer, BGGR, black 256, white
4095) and develops it with the camera's own tuning file (`imx477.json`, the Pi's
copy): black level, lens shading (`rpi.alsc`, the colour tables divided by their
minimum as libcamera does), digital gain and white balance from the record,
OpenCV demosaic, the colour matrix for the shot's colour temperature (`rpi.ccm`),
then a tone curve. The steps were checked against libcamera's source.

Gallery `artifacts/raw-base-gallery/` (154 photographs: the 141 training sources
and 13 held-out scenes; script `scripts/experiments/raw_base_gallery.py`):

- With the ISP's own tone curve (`isp`) the developed DNG matches the camera's
  JPEG: signed difference +1.5 / +0.4 / +1.3 levels (R / G / B) on average, within
  2 levels in every light, no lens-shading difference in brightness, corners about
  2 % bluer in daylight (the ISP's lens shading adapts per scene; ours does not).
- The absolute difference rises from 3 to 4 levels in daylight to 11 to 14 at
  night: the camera denoises, the developer does not.
- 8 bits hold what the raw has: shadow crops pushed +2 stops and highlight crops
  pushed -2 stops look the same from the 8-bit and the float development. In the
  shadows sensor noise, not quantisation, is the limit; with the highlight-
  protecting exposure the raw is rarely clipped, so there is little extra highlight
  detail to recover.
- Two softer tone curves (`soft-1`, `soft-1.5`: shadows lifted 1 or 1.5 stops,
  highlights rolled off) were shown; the owner preferred `isp`.

On the Mac a 12 MP development takes 1.4 to 1.9 s and about 250 MB, most of it the
tone curve's interpolation and the colour matrix.

## Phase 2: training on it

The 141 sources developed with `isp` (`scripts/experiments/develop_folder.py`,
quality-95 JPEGs under the same names) were trained with exactly the command of
today's LUT:

```
pifilm-train --source data/source-imx477-v3-highlight-dng-isp \
  --target data/references-merged-2026-09-29 --out artifacts/imx477-dng-isp-v1 \
  --no-source-white-balance --source-lift-highlight-ref 0.02 \
  --source-gamma-min 0.65 --source-gamma-max 1.25 --allow-small
```

The same references, the same validation split (113 / 28 sources), every gate
passed. Held-out distance to the Parr references (sliced Wasserstein, lower is
closer):

| Input | Before grading | After grading |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera JPEG (today's LUT, `98e36716`) | 0.0285 | 0.0187 |
| Developed DNG (`imx477-dng-isp-v1`, `24f5b91f`) | 0.0331 | 0.0213 |

The developed DNGs start further from the Parr look (no adaptive contrast, no
sharpening) and end further from it too.

The raw cube was given the deployed look's normalisation and grain
(`artifacts/imx477-dng-isp-v1-dark-tile`) and both graded with the same grain seed
per photograph: gallery `artifacts/raw-look-gallery/` (script
`scripts/experiments/raw_look_gallery.py`). By eye the raw look is a touch lighter
and softer in the shadows and very slightly warmer indoors, otherwise the same.

## Why there is no gain

With the camera's own tone curve, the developed DNG is the camera's JPEG without
its denoise, sharpening and adaptive contrast, and 8 bits already hold everything
the raw adds at these exposures. The raw would pay off with a different tone (the
soft curves) or when exposure is pushed afterwards, neither of which is wanted.

## If it is revisited

The DNGs and records of 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-12 are kept (Mac:
`~/Downloads/PiTrainingSource/`, `~/Downloads/PiCameraPhotos/`; Nextcloud). The
developer, the gallery scripts and this command reproduce everything here. What
was not built: developing on the Pi (phase 3 would measure it; perhaps 7 to 15 s a
shot), and wiring a raw look into capture (phase 4: an `input` field in
`params.json`, refusing cropped or flipped sensor modes).
